This workshop offers a practical, experiential approach to emotional well-being — integrating gentle somatic practices, reflective writing, and guided exploration.

Designed to help you regulate your nervous system, shift perspective, and access a deeper sense of clarity and alignment in daily life.

Why This Work Matters

Many of us try to "figure things out" mentally — while deeper patterns in the body and nervous system continue to shape how we feel and respond.

This workshop bridges that gap, offering a way to work with both mind and body in an integrated, practical way.

What You'll Gain

01

Shift habitual emotional patterns that no longer serve you

02

Develop a more regulated, settled internal state

03

Access wider, more supportive perspectives on your experience

04

Respond with clarity and intention instead of reactivity

What to Expect

  • Gentle, accessible somatic practices

  • Guided reflective writing exercises

  • Clear frameworks grounded in neuroscience

  • Optional group sharing in a thoughtful, low-pressure space

No prior experience with yoga, writing, or meditation is needed.

About Adi

Adi Maron-Katz holds a PhD in neuroscience with extensive experience in the field of mental health, alongside years of teaching yoga and other somatic and contemplative practices.

She guides individuals and groups through integrative processes that bridge scientific understanding and embodied experience to create grounded, practical approaches to emotional well-being.
